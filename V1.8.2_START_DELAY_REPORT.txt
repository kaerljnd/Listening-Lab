Listening Lab v1.8.2 startup-delay update

Changes:
1. New practice sessions wait 3 seconds before the first audio starts.
2. Visible 3-second preparation countdown in the practice UI.
3. Best Response options are not exposed during the preparation countdown.
4. Set detail page explains the 3-second startup delay under both full-module and question-type modes.
5. Refresh recovery during the preparation phase restarts from the saved remaining countdown instead of getting stuck.
6. Pause and question review are disabled during the initial preparation countdown; Exit remains available in question-type mode.
7. The delay happens once per new session, not before every later material.
